# Gemma 4 26B-A4B experiments

The 26B-A4B shares the Gemma 4 engine with the 31B; its attention, sampling
and MTP decisions are in the [31B experiments](../gemma-4-31b/EXPERIMENTS.md).
The rows below cover the expert layers. Decode rates are `gufo bench` tg128
on UD-Q4_K_XL unless stated; the draft A/Bs are HTTP (`tools/gemma4/draft_policy_bench.py`).

CR2 (review P2, Minor-but-real) tests/extensions/ext_runtime_neutral_contracts_test.cpp —
`Expect(ceiling * 2 > budget && ceiling < budget * 8)` fails on any host or

two independent live samples, with a 256 MiB drift allowance, and an explicit
